Historical matched accuracy population

128 pressure requests from 64 teacher simulations, at two recorded frames per simulation. Small grids contain 150–4,096 cells, with 8 or 16 cells per metre. Uniform water density is 1,000 kg/m³ and the timestep is 1/60 s on sealed regular grids. These tests do not establish performance on arbitrary geometry, obstacles or free surfaces. Fresh and larger-domain benchmarks are separate selections.

A common test population is necessary for comparison, but it does not make different timing procedures equivalent. The historical speed scatter remains exploratory. Automatic recommendations use one recorded capture and population at a time. The Pareto line is monotonic by construction; its shape alone cannot identify a cause of the accuracy–speed relationship.
