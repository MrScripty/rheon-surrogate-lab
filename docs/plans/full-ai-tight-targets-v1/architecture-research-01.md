# Full-AI tight-target architecture research 01

Research date: 2026-10-20. Status: design evidence only; no training, numerical model execution, or timing was performed for this note. Earlier completed-study files were read without modification. External references below are papers, authors' repositories, or official library documentation.

## Decision and scope

Prioritize a shared learned endpoint/intermediate decoder and a narrow learned spectral operator as two alternative architectural mechanisms. Keep a learned multiscale latent processor as the fallback, with cross-block feature reuse tested separately only after a stateless generator qualifies. None of the reviewed sources establishes the combination of one-thread CPU latency, complete 3D pressure/velocity/tracer states, worst residual, intermediate-state accuracy, and long own-history accuracy required here. These are falsifiable experiments, not a forecast that the targets are attainable.

The controlling [activation](activation-01.json) requires K = 1, 2, 3, 4 complete future states per pass at provisional reference size 32 × 32 × 32; maximum observed full-block/K must be at most 1.2 ms, hence maximum block budgets 1.2/2.4/3.6/4.8 ms. Worst equation residual and pressure/velocity/tracer relative L2 must each be at most 1e-5 at every output and throughout 240 actual own-history steps. Retain the smaller odd rectangular regressions. Native numerical solvers remain offline teachers/controls; candidate inference may contain neither a numerical pressure solve nor a corrective projection. Density-50/100 training is gated on all four phase-1 successes; larger domains come next; 600-step tests at 60/30 Hz follow the larger-domain gate.

Completed 32-cubed medians are roughly 89–105 ms/state, while their worst errors remain approximately 1e-3. The small-grid 1.77–1.84 ms/state medians do not establish the new maximum-latency or accuracy contract. Historical `accuracy_pass`/`target_met` flags in the activation's copied baseline rows describe their earlier contracts, not new 1e-5/1.2-ms success. See [reserved costs](../full-ai-next-priorities-v1/temporal-reserved-cost-results.json).

The current model's 100,456 total parameters include only 6,600 trainable temporal parameters in the fixed-viscosity family. The total count therefore describes neither freely trained full-state capacity nor runtime work. [Parameter inventory](../full-ai-next-priorities-v1/temporal-displacement-results.json), [fixed-viscosity qualification contract](../full-ai-next-priorities-v1/fixed-viscosity-qualification-protocol-01.json).

Source inspection already shows one encoding per block, followed by sequential hidden updates, displacement, viscosity, learned pressure, and momentum readouts for every horizon. The learned pressure route for larger geometry retains its source component; small-geometry dense learned-map compilation is a separate route. Merely adding encoder sharing would repeat an existing optimization. Replacing expensive repeated paths, or performing learned evolution in a cheaper representation, is the relevant hypothesis. [Displacement model](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/bundle_model_displacement.py), [pressure map](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/full_learned_pressure_map.py), [fixed-viscosity wrapper](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/bundle_model_fixed_viscosity.py).

The route discontinuity is concrete: dense unit-response maps are built only when `max(counts) <= 16`, subject to a 512 MiB total cache cap. A 32-cubed request instead runs `FrozenPressureCore`, with two learned correction stages per physical output. Its fine U-Net is composed of linear convolutions/identity/pooling/nearest upsampling, but low/tail proposals also have residual-norm acceptance guards; do not assume the complete routed map is one unconditional linear operator. A dense 32-cubed response matrix would require 32,768² × 8 bytes = 8 GiB in float64 for one geometry, before workspace/output storage. Enlarging dense caches therefore does not provide the desired voxel-linear scaling. Prefer structured learned global operators, conditional on measured runtime and error floors. [Learned-map route and cache limits](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/full_learned_pressure_map.py), [frozen spatial graph and guards](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/frozen_temporal_pressure_core.py).

## What the primary literature supports

**Learned spectral operators.** FNO applies a Fourier transform, learned mode/channel weights, an inverse transform, a local linear branch, and nonlinearities. This supplies global communication without dense all-pairs spatial multiplication. Its Navier–Stokes example is 2D vorticity; “FNO-3D” there means two spatial axes plus time, not complete states on a 3D spatial grid. Table 1 reports errors including 0.0086, well above 1e-5; its advertised 0.005-second inference comparison runs on GPU. These results establish an architectural possibility, not this CPU/error target. [FNO, §§4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.08895v3).

F-FNO replaces multidimensional spectral mixing with separable per-axis spectral layers and improved residual connections. This is a useful bounded alternative to an expensive dense 3D spectral weight tensor; the paper also studies noise and Markov-state training. Axis factorization reduces representation complexity but restricts a single layer's cross-axis coupling, so it may need depth that consumes the saved runtime. Do not assume its reported speed/accuracy transfers to the present geometry and worst-case metrics. [F-FNO, §§3–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.13802v3), [authors' code](https://github.com/alasdairtran/fourierflow).

The permitted distinction should be explicit: `inverse_transform(learned_coefficients * transform(features))` is a learned layer. FFT/DCT and inverse FFT/DCT are fixed changes of basis; an inverse *transform* is not a pressure inverse. In contrast, inserting eigenvalue reciprocals of the pressure operator, running an iterative pressure solve, or projecting an already predicted velocity would violate this program. A trained coefficient may approximate an inverse relationship; its provenance must remain learning, with no analytically generated inverse weights or hidden solver fallback. This boundary follows the activation contract, not a claim that a spectral basis alone confers physical correctness.

A DCT gives a real-valued basis with selectable definitions and normalization. Its exact transform type, sample locations, reflection extension, and inverse must be registered; “use a DCT” does not establish the wall treatment for staggered velocity and cell-centered pressure. The current padded input also has size count+1, so even a 32-cell axis may present a 33-sample network tensor. Transform cost and semantics must use the actual layout. SciPy documents DCT normalization and `workers`; FFT performance also depends on factorization of transform lengths. Set workers/threads explicitly and retain odd shapes rather than silently replacing their domain by a convenient power of two. [SciPy DCT](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.fft.dct.html), [SciPy FFT](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.fft.fft.html), [current codec](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/fast_neural_state_codec.py).

**Temporal sharing.** MP-PDE predicts a bundle of future time states synchronously and trains on its own perturbed distribution using the pushforward method: earlier unrolls supply inputs, with gradients through the final unroll. Its experiments support studying both bundling and rollout-aware training, but its reported GPU rollout times and survival/error criteria do not certify 1e-5 CPU rollouts. Adopt these ideas without adopting graph-message-passing overhead on this regular grid. [MP-PDE, §§3.1, 4.2 and Appendix F](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.03376v3), [authors' implementation](https://github.com/brandstetter-johannes/MP-Neural-PDE-Solvers).

An accurate endpoint does not determine every intermediate trajectory under changing forcing, and linear interpolation need not satisfy the discrete equations. Endpoint-conditioned intermediate decoding is consequently a hypothesis requiring direct supervision at all K outputs. Reuse features from the initial state and endpoint, condition on horizon and the prescribed force sequence, and learn the temporal detail rather than accepting interpolated states as a control-quality result. No future reference state may enter inference. This is a proposed design, not a conclusion proved by temporal bundling.

**Multiscale alternatives.** Multiwavelet operator learning learns kernels across fixed wavelet scales; CNO adapts a U-Net with controlled resampling/nonlinearities to address aliasing in operator learning. Both motivate preserving fine information while doing expensive processing at coarse scales. CNO also supplies empirical evidence that resolution-invariant parameterization alone does not guarantee invariant error. Neither publication certifies this task's odd rectangular staggered geometry or 1e-5 rollout requirement. [Multiwavelet operator, §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.13459v2), [CNO, §2 and resolution experiments](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.01178v3).

**Stability and precision.** PDE-Refiner identifies neglected low-amplitude frequencies as a source of long-rollout degradation and uses multiple learned refinement passes. This supports inspecting error by spatial band, including small-amplitude detail. Its iterative decoder is not an immediate latency recommendation here; every extra pass must fit the full-block budget. Correlation/survival-time success in a paper cannot replace checking worst per-field errors at each of 240 states. [PDE-Refiner, §§2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.05732v2).

Float32 has machine epsilon about 1.19e-7, which is not a bound on a model's final error. Differencing, cancellation, reductions, normalization, and repeated codec roundings can consume the margin to 1e-5. PyTorch explicitly warns that equivalent computations and batching/order choices need not produce bitwise-equal floating-point results. Use typed restored CPU outputs for acceptance; try selected float64 accumulations only if measured error attribution justifies them. Lower-precision training can remain separate from deployment, but quantization/float16 inference should not be presumed admissible. [NumPy `finfo`](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/generated/numpy.finfo.html), [PyTorch numerical accuracy](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/notes/numerical_accuracy.html), [deployed codec roundings](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/fast_neural_state_codec.py).

## Four bounded candidate experiments

These bounds are proposed preregistration choices. They do not start new optimizer updates. Freeze scene splits, exact field/scaling/error contracts, training budgets, and implementation inventories before fitting. Perform candidate construction/cost qualification only after the ongoing frozen-component diagnosis identifies the costs and error floors.

| ID | Bounded change | Why it might help | Principal risk and falsification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: endpoint plus learned intermediates | Encode once; one learned endpoint trunk predicts all endpoint fields. A shared decoder emits each earlier state from initial features, endpoint latent, normalized horizon, and known forcing. Compare exactly two decoders: direct joint horizon heads versus endpoint-conditioned heads; use the same trunk and training budget. Start width 8 with at most two expensive spatial trunk blocks and pointwise horizon heads. Include K=1 as a real candidate. | Removes the repeated pressure/state-generation path rather than just sharing the encoder; earlier outputs reuse the expensive endpoint representation. | Endpoint accuracy may hide impossible intermediates or require an equally expensive temporal decoder. Reject if any intermediate field/residual exceeds 1e-5 or if the full block exceeds its K budget. Endpoint-only success is failure. An offline oracle-endpoint decoder control can isolate intermediate representational limits, but never counts as candidate inference. |
| B: narrow learned spectral full-state trunk | Alternative to A's trunk: at most two operator blocks, width 8, learned axis-factorized mixing plus a local detail branch. Register two mode capacities, e.g. 8 and all available modes capped to each actual axis. Select one boundary-aware FFT/DCT convention after layout review; do not multiply the search by unregistered basis variants. Joint K-field heads, no pressure solve/projection. | Global interactions could replace a repeated deep pressure path; shared coefficients/features can serve all horizons. | Transform/layout overhead can dominate; mode truncation may erase residual-critical detail; separability can underfit coupled directions. Reject on actual one-thread full-block time, wall/odd-shape error, or high-frequency residual floor. All-mode failure distinguishes capacity/precision problems from simple truncation. |
| C: learned coarse latent stepping with fine detail | One two-level encoder (one factor-2 reduction, width 8 fine/16 coarse), a tied learned latent update run K times at coarse resolution, one shared lightweight decoder per output with initial fine skip features. Compare exactly two fine readouts: pointwise only versus one depthwise spatial layer plus pointwise mixing. | Moves repeated temporal work to fewer cells while retaining initial fine structure; can use shared temporal weights. | Fine detail evolves and an initial skip may become stale; cheap upsampling may alias or miss wall layers. Reject if the decoder floor persists with an offline teacher latent/control, if any fine-band or odd-shape output fails, or if K=1/full-block timing fails. Fixed restriction/prolongation is representation, not permission to add a multigrid pressure solve. |
| D: cross-block feature reuse, conditional on A/B/C | Take one stateless architecture only. Cache geometry/transform plans freely as setup; separately compare fresh dynamic encoding versus a learned hidden-state carry with a fixed refresh interval of 1 or 4 blocks. Train the carry on its own state distribution. Time refresh blocks and cache reset, including worst observed block. | Reuses earlier learned features across generated blocks where temporal changes are small. | Hidden state can encode stale dynamics, hide extra initial work, or make restarts inconsistent. Reject on worst refresh latency, 240-step error, or reset/restart inconsistency. Invalidate on scene/state replacement, geometry, forcing-contract, timestep, viscosity, device, or precision changes. A cache containing reference-derived future features is disallowed. |

Do not expand all four into an unrestricted architecture search. Profile-guided ordering: A first if repeated field/pressure paths dominate, B first if global pressure communication dominates and transform primitives fit; C is the alternative if the spectral boundary/cost tradeoff fails. D stays gated on a qualifying stateless architecture. The original generic [joint-head model](../../../experiments/full-ai-next-priorities-v1/bundle_model.py) already existed, so A must be compared against its actual earlier failures and the completed-study diagnostics rather than presented as a wholly new idea.

## Common acceptance and early stopping

1. **Immutable inference contract.** Export a finite learned graph and typed checkpoint inventory. Audit for pressure inverses, eigenvalue reciprocals, numerical/projection fallbacks, adaptive solver loops, teacher access, and uncharged preprocessing. Predict complete physical fields using the correct staggered locations, boundary masks, and pressure gauge.
2. **Cost before a managed fit.** Using the parent task's controlled profiling protocol, qualify actual tensor shapes, layout conversions, allocations, transforms, output materialization, and the deployed codec. If mandatory work alone exceeds the block budget, stop that candidate before training. Encoder-only, endpoint-only, amortized warm-cache, and median timings are diagnostic scopes, never the acceptance scope. Report full-block median/p95/maximum and maximum block/K on a fixed one-thread population; separate setup and complete-application costs.
3. **Measure attainable error floors.** Use offline exact-component controls and training-scene overfit checks to distinguish immutable frozen-map, field representation, precision/codec, and optimization floors. An exact teacher component used here is diagnostic and cannot appear in the scored inference path. A frozen component whose measured downstream floor exceeds 1e-5 needs replacement or retraining; more temporal head training alone is not evidence it can overcome that floor.
4. **Supervise every horizon.** Train state and discrete residual objectives at every output, with training-only fixed field/near-zero normalization scales and explicit absolute tolerances registered in advance. Include frequency/derivative-aware diagnostics because small state L2 error need not imply small discrete residual. Do not repair predictions during evaluation. PDE-residual losses are training objectives; they are not hard constraints or proof of accuracy. [PINO's combination of data and physics training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.03794) is relevant to this distinction, without adopting its inference-time optimization options.
5. **Own-history test.** Run each K generator for 240 actual steps, feeding its own emitted endpoint and, where applicable, hidden state to the next block. Score all intermediates, not only block endpoints. Preregister direct-horizon versus composed-block consistency as a diagnostic; neither self-consistency nor contraction alone proves agreement with the native reference. Include changing forcing, small/near-zero fields, walls, and retained odd rectangular shapes. Any threshold violation fails the candidate even if population averages improve.
6. **No target relaxation by source analogy.** More trainable parameters, a learned global basis, fast GPU papers, visually plausible flow, and long stable rollouts are each insufficient. Stop or revise a bounded candidate when its finite evidence falsifies the target; retain failed evidence and wait for a new registered mechanism before expanding training. The later density and 600-step work remain gated as specified in the activation.
