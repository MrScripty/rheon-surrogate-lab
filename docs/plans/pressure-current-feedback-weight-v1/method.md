# Measurement method

The controlled change is current_feedback.weight0.01/0.03 on frozen native late
bank02. Original band_feedback.weight stays0.03. The retained current0.1 model
has the same optimizer work; the older no-current0.03 model has a different work
budget and is context only. No architecture, CFD data, physical contract,
normalization, initialization, sampling or update-schedule change is admitted.

Each new managed CUDA Study must finish5,000updates, publish verified artifacts
and restore on CPU with CUDA hidden. Five numerical artifact pairs must match
exactly. The matched-training report also verifies actual50,000forwards and
425703026forward cells, original stream histories, current sampling, source/input

qualified custody-monitor change is checked against current owner bytes and AST
equality outside its output-monitor function; numerical sources must remain exact.

Each CPU package first passes the one-frame physical fixture. Its74whole-scene
development campaign then retains paired trajectories and233independent sparse
pressure checks, with15native warm-up steps, valid previous pressure and at most
32actual network calls. Mean-free independent pressure/gradient error and paired
trajectory pressure deviation are different measurements. Apply original58,
late-amended8 and minimum8-failed8 pressure/mean/max-call gates independently
against all three historical comparators. Only an eligible, frozen candidate may
touch the eight unused whole scenes. Physical qualification alone is insufficient.

Transfer diagnostics use the already consumed grids33x11x9,16x16x16 and25x16x23,
with cubic h=.125m, rho1000kg/m3 and dt1/60s. Stationary cosine pressure modes
1/2/3 along the longest array axis are tested at1/.001/1e-8Pa. Mixed fields add
mode7at1Pa; high-only fields have no intentional low-mode signal. Seven fields
per low mode and grid give63cases, each with12sequential single-evaluation
proposals and snapshots after1/4/8/12calls:756forwards,252rows per checkpoint.
Every correction recomputes the physical residual. No inverse/native solver or
spectral correction supplies the prediction.

Independent cosine eigenvalues verify RHS assembly. Orthonormal separable DCT
Parseval checks verify absolute pressure energy, in Pa2per cell, for every field
and snapshot. The low band includes per-axis modes0..3, excluding DC. Pressure
error removes the constant gauge; gradient error uses internal face differences
divided by h; equation error uses the sealed physical operator. High-only low-mode
coefficients and low-band energy measure leakage. Pure/mixed/high-only fields
and amplitudes remain separate in summaries; every case tail stays in CSV.
These stationary measurements cannot establish temporal aliasing, accumulated
trajectory drift or a causal noise floor.

One-thread transfer diagnostics use core23,2GiBaccounting plus8GiBavailable reserve,
reserve rechecks and bounded address-space growth. Broad CPU campaigns/common-RHS
pools use16singlethread workers and never overlap each other. They may overlap
the next CUDA fit. The idle qualification waiter was explicitly stopped before
any child work and replaced with an owner following actual CUDA order003then001.
Full Study and CPU phase clocks are retained separately from optimizer time.

Serial timing follows all broad pools: two reversed-order repetitions/model,
one core22, four consumed cubic sizes16/24/32/40,15native warm-up plus8AIsteps.
Repeated physical arrays and call counts must match exactly. Timed AI-branch
cost includes the additional same-request native diagnostic solve, capture,
correction, IPC and logging; external sparse oracles are absent. These timings
do not measure standalone AI/native solver speed, and reference_seconds must
not be subtracted as an estimate of the diagnostic solve.

The original Tuldok catalog owns Models/Studies/Runs. Existing observation paths
show completed pressure frames once available; they do not represent live
velocity or tracer animation. Source, methods, small receipts, failures and
summary CSV/JSON are committed. Weights, arrays, runtime and credentials stay
local. Independent source reviews and actual evidence review remain distinct.
