Method, evidence and limits

After four passes, low spatial modes contained over 99.7% of the remaining pressure-error energy in C53/C55. Very weak original coefficients contributed less than 0.002% of that error. This screening targets coherent low-mode corrections rather than assuming noise is the main cause.

Float32 neural compute; float64 residual calculation and pressure reconstruction. The unchanged acceptance target is 1e-6 relative to the full pressure-equation right-hand side. Added training feedback uses the initial correction-request residual as its normalization denominator. Snapshot timing covers recurrence and the final equation check, excluding input assembly and IPC. Independent simulations run in bounded parallel CPU workers, so their elapsed time is not isolated inference latency.
