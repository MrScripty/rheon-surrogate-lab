4. Numerical coarse solve plus neural fine correction: solve pressure accurately on a coarse grid, then train the network to recover the remaining fine-grid detail.
5. Sparse numerical anchors plus neural infill: calculate selected pressure information numerically and predict the rest. Include the cost of obtaining those anchors.
6. A revised fully learned coarse/fine architecture: test a specific improvement over the learned coarse branch already tried.
7. Broader spatial-resolution training: vary cells per metre separately from domain cell counts, then test whether explicit spacing conditioning adds value.
8. Periodic numerical correction: measure whether occasional numerical solves control accumulated drift at a worthwhile total cost.

Across these, we’ll measure worst error versus total runtime, small-grid regressions, and how long independently advanced simulations remain within a declared accuracy tolerance. Larger deployment sizes—256³, 512³ and 1024³—come later.
