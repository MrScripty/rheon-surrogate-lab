Interpretation and limits

The comparisons isolate data retention, global learned structure and numerical coarse correction. Extra numerical stages are timed and are not FLOP-matched to the fine network. The repeated seeds change sampling order while preserving initialization. Larger-grid native failures remain measured negatives even where manufactured equations pass.

The full spectral solver exploits sealed domains with uniform cubic cells and constant fluid mass density. Cells per metre describes spatial sampling density and is separate from fluid mass density. Obstacles, free surfaces and variable coefficients require a different operator contract. Native CG labels have finite error, so apparent pressure improvements near that error floor require the independent equation residual for interpretation.

No model was promoted. Original C37, C44 and C53 artifact identities are verified. Every successful fit has a separate fresh CPU restore with artifact parity. One rejected launch failed deadline validation before any optimizer update.
