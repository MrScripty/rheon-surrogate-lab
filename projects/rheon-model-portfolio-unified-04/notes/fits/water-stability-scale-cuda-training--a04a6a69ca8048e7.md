# water-stability-scale · CUDA training

Retrospective source review · fit-a04a6a69ca8048e7

Authored interpretation of captured source and receipts. No new training, benchmark, lineage or scientific promotion is implied.

**Method.** A 370,016-parameter multiscale 3D CNN learns a pressure correction from one channel: q=(rho/h)*equation residual, divided by its train-only RMS. Grid shape supplies the sealed cubic Neumann geometry; density and spacing act through the physical rescaling. Timestep is validated but is not a separate neural channel. The F32 core has encoder widths 8/16/24/32, bottleneck 48 and decoder widths 32/24/16/8, with no GroupNorm. SiLU and every convolution bias are removed; inherited activation=silu metadata does not describe those executed layers. The configured policy allows 4 correction(s) per pressure request, recomputing the F64 equation residual between passes. The F32 correction is scaled, cell-zero gauged and added to supplied pressure with F64 arithmetic. This is not a simulation advance and does not accept independent obstacle/free-surface masks.

**Purpose.** Scale learned corrections by the training correction RMS rather than previous-pressure RMS, without manufactured feedback.

**Experiment.** 5,000 whole-network Adam updates, batch 1, initial rate 0.001, schedule [{'learning_rate': 0.0001, 'update': 3000}, {'learning_rate': 1e-05, 'update': 4500}], seed 20261015 and clipping at norm 1.0. One physical training frame at native index 15, 16x16x16 cells and 8 cells/metre; 64 validation trajectories provide 128 frames at 8/16 cells per metre. Physical timestep is 1/60 s. The physical-frame loss equally weights normalized pressure-field and equation errors. Output uses the train correction RMS (2.1793165157463443 Pa). No manufactured feedback executes. 

**Observed outcome.** Last logged training objective 5.2345423911609511e-08; retained final validation objective 0.00079406457676027282. Common 128-request worst mean-free pressure relative L2 0.079388778284669614; equation relative L2 0.14641367487627485. Complete GPU pressure-call timing: median of case medians 9.0236884999999987 ms, worst case median 10.0107365 ms. The boundary includes native CPU arrays/packing/transfers/output/synchronization; setup and scoring are excluded. This is not per-simulation-step latency or an own-history drift result.

**Lesson.** Output scale changes improve common pressure/equation errors over the prior four-correction baseline without adding scenes. The configuration declares 32 feedback patterns but weight zero; the saved history confirms no manufactured bank executed.

Manufactured feedback is regenerated by the captured analytical.py from the training frame shape/scalars/correction amplitude and its private RNG seed. Historical training ends with CPU deployment/parity forwards; a clean GPU-only reproducer must first be qualified under the current policy. Keep old-Rheon data separate from new-solver captures. No clean retraining, optimizer resume or new-Rheon regeneration is qualified by this review.
