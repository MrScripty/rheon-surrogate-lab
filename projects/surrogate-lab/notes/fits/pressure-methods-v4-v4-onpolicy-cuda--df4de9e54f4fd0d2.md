# Pressure methods v4 · v4-onpolicy CUDA

Retrospective source review · fit-df4de9e54f4fd0d2

Authored interpretation of captured source and receipts. No new training, benchmark, lineage or scientific promotion is implied.

**Method.** A 371,214-parameter F32 linear multiscale pressure CNN plus learned global branch: 370,016 fine coefficients, 63 coarse logits and a 1,135-parameter grid-shape head. Two ordinary internal pressure corrections each apply a residual-gated low-mode cosine proposal and fine neural correction accumulated in F64. Physical q=(rho/h)r is the single neural field input, using fixed C53 scales; dt is scalar validation metadata. This is a pressure-only request, not two physical simulation outputs.

**Purpose.** Add four native captures produced under the learned pressure policy and spectrally relabel their requests, testing training exposure to states reached by the candidate policy.

**Experiment.** 2,400 CUDA Adam updates, batch one, clip norm one; fine rate 1e-05 with captured late drops. There are 38 parent groups / 84 saved examples, all train-only. The sampler preserves 1,600 small draws and interleaves 800 larger-tier draws; exact parent/frame exposures and voxel work verify. Grids extend through 128³ at 8/16/32 cells per metre, with fixed dt=1/60 s and fluid density 1,000 kg/m³. Both start from exact completed C101 weights. The original 34-parent bank is retained. Four added captures use the learned pressure policy, with four snapshots each at native indices 0/15/59/119; exact spectral request labels are retained separately. The unchanged final/intermediate physical equation-feedback objective uses two neural proposals, early weight 0.05, target 1e-6 and no eight-pass teacher. Captured learning work is 4,800 root neural forwards / 1,224,897,366 input voxels; inherited auxiliary banks and the old teacher are inactive.

**Observed outcome.** Retained final logged training loss 19.997731611636997. The existing common 128-request result has worst mean-free pressure relative L2 0.0052733128456422685, equation relative L2 0.00086335440399946733 and GPU median of case-median pressure calls 6.2170030000000001 ms. Scope: Complete captured two-correction pressure proposal; no velocity/tracer advance. Timings include host-owned input/output, validation/packing, transfers and synchronization for the captured readout, with setup/scoring separate. These are retained consumed small-grid cases, not independent large-grid or trajectory results. No new model execution occurs here.

**Lesson.** On-policy here denotes frozen captured states generated by a learned policy and then relabeled, not fresh online data collection during every optimizer update. Four parents contribute 16 snapshots. Their later temporal coverage does not make this pressure-only model a variable-timestep generator.

**Reproduction.** Retain the exact typed native recipe, all witnessed source/config/runtime, fixed normalization, complete original input packet, source population and initial/final tensors. Preserve balanced private RNG streams, physical parent/frame counts and final-update selection. Retain native augmentation receipts or manufactured-pattern generator as applicable; post-coarse and differentiable hierarchy training require their exact numerical arithmetic and gauge/adjoint. The historical wrapper/restore runs CPU neural inference and needs a qualified GPU-only route before replay. Clean retraining, new-Rheon regeneration, complete-composition benchmarking and drift remain unqualified; no historical Trial ancestry is invented.
