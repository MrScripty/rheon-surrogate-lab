# water-direction-late · CUDA training

Retrospective source review · fit-219479e596771b32

Authored interpretation of captured source and receipts. No new training, benchmark, lineage or scientific promotion is implied.

**Method.** A one-channel multiscale 3D convolutional pressure correction network, encoder widths 8/16/24/32 and bottleneck48. Actual captured transforms remove GroupNorm, convolution biases and SiLU activations for the linear correction policy; the inherited activation=silu metadata does not describe those transformed layers. The F32 network consumes the scaled pressure-equation residual q=(rho/h)r with train-only RMS normalization. F64 finite-volume residual assembly and cell-zero-gauge reconstruction wrap each correction. Timestep is validated at the call boundary but is not a separate neural input channel in this physical pressure formulation.

**Purpose.** Improve two-correction contraction on difficult residual directions while retaining primary and supplemental manufactured-shape feedback.

**Experiment.** Train the same 370,016-parameter seeded network for 5,000 Adam updates, batch size one, using 16 physical training trajectories / 32 frames and 2 pressure correction(s) per prediction. The primary manufactured bank contains 1,024 grid-pattern pairs across eight grids, including up to 40 cubed. Add 768 supplemental grid-pattern pairs on six rectangular grids and late captured directions. All parents share identical initial tensor inventories; several training factors and the C37 deployment work differ, so their ranking is not a one-factor causal ablation.

**Observed outcome.** C44, at its saved 2-correction default on the frozen 128 consumed requests: worst mean-free pressure relative L2 0.01345071709096545; equation relative L2 0.0039826222242511887. The retained matrix additionally measures one/two/four/eight corrections. No fresh timing or trajectory-drift result belongs to this review.

**Lesson.** Separate physical CFD frames, manufactured spatial patterns and captured residual directions when describing training diversity. These parents retain a much broader fitting stream than the later eight-pattern detached fine-tunes. C37 continuations improve only conditionally on metric and correction budget, while the C44/C53 continuations regress against their own parents at every matched tested budget. This motivates preserving broad real/directional rehearsal during future correction tuning, but does not identify narrow fitting as the sole cause. Manufactured large grids do not establish high-density or large-domain physical-trajectory accuracy.

**Reproduction.** Use the exact captured project config, train-recipe.json, complete source witness, input-slot bindings and runtime manifest. Training starts from the recorded seeded checkpoint and saves the final configured update; optimizer resume is not captured. Keep the frozen old-Rheon data epoch distinct from simulations generated by the new solver. This source/receipt review and unchanged-checkpoint GPU replay do not qualify clean dataset regeneration or retraining.
