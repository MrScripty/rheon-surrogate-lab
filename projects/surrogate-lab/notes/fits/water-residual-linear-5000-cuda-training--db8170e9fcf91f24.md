# water-residual-linear-5000 · CUDA training

Retrospective source review · fit-db8170e9fcf91f24

Authored interpretation of captured source and receipts. No new training, benchmark, lineage or scientific promotion is implied.

**Method.** A 370,016-parameter multiscale 3D CNN learns a pressure correction from one channel: q=(rho/h)*equation residual, divided by its train-only RMS. Grid shape supplies the sealed cubic Neumann geometry; density and spacing act through the physical rescaling. Timestep is validated but is not a separate neural channel. The F32 core has encoder widths 8/16/24/32, bottleneck 48 and decoder widths 32/24/16/8, with no GroupNorm. SiLU and every convolution bias are removed; inherited activation=silu metadata does not describe those executed layers. The configured policy allows 1 correction(s) per pressure request, recomputing the F64 equation residual between passes. The F32 correction is scaled, cell-zero gauged and added to supplied pressure with F64 arithmetic. This is not a simulation advance and does not accept independent obstacle/free-surface masks.

**Purpose.** Provide a 5,000-update single-correction control for the recurrent model, with the same shorter learning-rate schedule.

**Experiment.** 5,000 whole-network Adam updates, batch 1, initial rate 0.001, schedule [{'learning_rate': 0.0001, 'update': 3000}, {'learning_rate': 1e-05, 'update': 4500}], seed 20261015 and clipping at norm 1.0. One physical training frame at native index 15, 16x16x16 cells and 8 cells/metre; 64 validation trajectories provide 128 frames at 8/16 cells per metre. Physical timestep is 1/60 s. The physical-frame loss equally weights normalized pressure-field and equation errors. Output uses the train previous-pressure RMS (26.41425532430595 Pa). No manufactured feedback executes. 

**Observed outcome.** Last logged training objective 9.15146209374817e-08; retained final validation objective 0.0059085585562438777. Common 128-request worst mean-free pressure relative L2 0.18021903457088728; equation relative L2 0.29136273875593766. Complete GPU pressure-call timing: median of case medians 2.0784832499999997 ms, worst case median 2.4445269999999999 ms. The boundary includes native CPU arrays/packing/transfers/output/synchronization; setup and scoring are excluded. This is not per-simulation-step latency or an own-history drift result.

**Lesson.** This is the short-budget control for C18, not merely a renamed C17 checkpoint. Its first 5,000 physical sample selections match the longer fit, but its learning-rate changes occur at different updates.
