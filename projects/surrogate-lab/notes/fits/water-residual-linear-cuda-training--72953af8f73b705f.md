# water-residual-linear · CUDA training

Retrospective source review · fit-72953af8f73b705f

Authored interpretation of captured source and receipts. No new training, benchmark, lineage or scientific promotion is implied.

**Method.** A 370,016-parameter multiscale 3D CNN learns a pressure correction from one channel: q=(rho/h)*equation residual, divided by its train-only RMS. Grid shape supplies the sealed cubic Neumann geometry; density and spacing act through the physical rescaling. Timestep is validated but is not a separate neural channel. The F32 core has encoder widths 8/16/24/32, bottleneck 48 and decoder widths 32/24/16/8, with no GroupNorm. SiLU and every convolution bias are removed; inherited activation=silu metadata does not describe those executed layers. The configured policy allows 1 correction(s) per pressure request, recomputing the F64 equation residual between passes. The F32 correction is scaled, cell-zero gauged and added to supplied pressure with F64 arithmetic. This is not a simulation advance and does not accept independent obstacle/free-surface masks.

**Purpose.** Make the correction network linear by removing SiLU and all convolution biases, matching the linear sealed pressure operator.

**Experiment.** 10,000 whole-network Adam updates, batch 1, initial rate 0.001, schedule [{'learning_rate': 0.0001, 'update': 1000}, {'learning_rate': 1e-05, 'update': 6000}], seed 20261015 and clipping at norm 1.0. One physical training frame at native index 15, 16x16x16 cells and 8 cells/metre; 64 validation trajectories provide 128 frames at 8/16 cells per metre. Physical timestep is 1/60 s. The physical-frame loss equally weights normalized pressure-field and equation errors. Output uses the train previous-pressure RMS (26.41425532430595 Pa). No manufactured feedback executes. 

**Observed outcome.** Last logged training objective 8.7992195285552492e-08; retained final validation objective 0.0047754594219673119. Common 128-request worst mean-free pressure relative L2 0.13604491673543817; equation relative L2 0.28998270311950897. Complete GPU pressure-call timing: median of case medians 2.0877872499999999 ms, worst case median 2.3210875 ms. The boundary includes native CPU arrays/packing/transfers/output/synchronization; setup and scoring are excluded. This is not per-simulation-step latency or an own-history drift result.

**Lesson.** The actual linear transform has 19 weight tensors and no biases or SiLU, despite inherited activation=silu metadata. Common worst pressure error improves versus C16, while worst equation error changes little. Preserve executed structure separately from inherited labels.

Manufactured feedback is regenerated by the captured analytical.py from the training frame shape/scalars/correction amplitude and its private RNG seed. Historical training ends with CPU deployment/parity forwards; a clean GPU-only reproducer must first be qualified under the current policy. Keep old-Rheon data separate from new-solver captures. No clean retraining, optimizer resume or new-Rheon regeneration is qualified by this review.
