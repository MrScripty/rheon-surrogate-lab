# water-residual-nonlinear · CUDA training

Retrospective source review · fit-7a4436f303915085

Authored interpretation of captured source and receipts. No new training, benchmark, lineage or scientific promotion is implied.

**Method.** A 370,433-parameter multiscale 3D CNN learns a pressure correction from one channel: q=(rho/h)*equation residual, divided by its train-only RMS. Grid shape supplies the sealed cubic Neumann geometry; density and spacing act through the physical rescaling. Timestep is validated but is not a separate neural channel. The F32 core has encoder widths 8/16/24/32, bottleneck 48 and decoder widths 32/24/16/8, with no GroupNorm. SiLU and convolution biases remain. The configured policy allows 1 correction(s) per pressure request, recomputing the F64 equation residual between passes. The F32 correction is scaled, cell-zero gauged and added to supplied pressure with F64 arithmetic. This is not a simulation advance and does not accept independent obstacle/free-surface masks.

**Purpose.** Replace seven feature channels with a physically scaled pressure-equation residual, retaining nonlinear SiLU corrections.

**Experiment.** 10,000 whole-network Adam updates, batch 1, initial rate 0.001, schedule [{'learning_rate': 0.0001, 'update': 1000}, {'learning_rate': 1e-05, 'update': 6000}], seed 20261015 and clipping at norm 1.0. One physical training frame at native index 15, 16x16x16 cells and 8 cells/metre; 64 validation trajectories provide 128 frames at 8/16 cells per metre. Physical timestep is 1/60 s. The physical-frame loss equally weights normalized pressure-field and equation errors. Output uses the train previous-pressure RMS (26.41425532430595 Pa). No manufactured feedback executes. 

**Observed outcome.** Last logged training objective 1.3569468662482497e-07; retained final validation objective 0.0042426918638385543. Common 128-request worst mean-free pressure relative L2 0.26086887838879469; equation relative L2 0.29100166907826802. Complete GPU pressure-call timing: median of case medians 2.1806505 ms, worst case median 2.4109105 ms. The boundary includes native CPU arrays/packing/transfers/output/synchronization; setup and scoring are excluded. This is not per-simulation-step latency or an own-history drift result.

**Lesson.** Removing irrelevant conditioning simplifies the pressure map but does not by itself resolve common worst errors. This is still fitting one physical frame; broad validation does not enlarge the optimizer scene population.
