# water-stability-modes · CUDA training

Retrospective source review · fit-ad7585ce51993b1d

Authored interpretation of captured source and receipts. No new training, benchmark, lineage or scientific promotion is implied.

**Method.** A 370,016-parameter multiscale 3D CNN learns a pressure correction from one channel: q=(rho/h)*equation residual, divided by its train-only RMS. Grid shape supplies the sealed cubic Neumann geometry; density and spacing act through the physical rescaling. Timestep is validated but is not a separate neural channel. The F32 core has encoder widths 8/16/24/32, bottleneck 48 and decoder widths 32/24/16/8, with no GroupNorm. SiLU and every convolution bias are removed; inherited activation=silu metadata does not describe those executed layers. The configured policy allows 4 correction(s) per pressure request, recomputing the F64 equation residual between passes. The F32 correction is scaled, cell-zero gauged and added to supplied pressure with F64 arithmetic. This is not a simulation advance and does not accept independent obstacle/free-surface masks.

**Purpose.** Add manufactured Neumann pressure modes and mixtures, including a penalty for residual amplification, to four-correction training.

**Experiment.** 5,000 whole-network Adam updates, batch 1, initial rate 0.001, schedule [{'learning_rate': 0.0001, 'update': 3000}, {'learning_rate': 1e-05, 'update': 4500}], seed 20261015 and clipping at norm 1.0. One physical training frame at native index 15, 16x16x16 cells and 8 cells/metre; 64 validation trajectories provide 128 frames at 8/16 cells per metre. Physical timestep is 1/60 s. The physical-frame loss equally weights normalized pressure-field and equation errors. Output uses the train previous-pressure RMS (26.41425532430595 Pa). Additional feedback uses 32 manufactured cosine patterns/mixtures at four signed amplitudes, giving 128 fields and 5,000 sampled extra proposals at weight 0.1. Its pressure/gradient/equation weights are 0.25/0.25/0.5 plus a residual-amplification penalty. These are operator examples, not CFD scenes. 

**Observed outcome.** Last logged training objective 2.0536081148872925e-06; retained final validation objective 1.3466672895588921e-05. Common 128-request worst mean-free pressure relative L2 0.016844521634938693; equation relative L2 0.032820812418264185. Complete GPU pressure-call timing: median of case medians 9.1233652499999991 ms, worst case median 9.8363650000000007 ms. The boundary includes native CPU arrays/packing/transfers/output/synchronization; setup and scoring are excluded. This is not per-simulation-step latency or an own-history drift result.

**Lesson.** Synthetic operator examples materially improve common errors despite using only one CFD frame. The 128 manufactured fields are 32 patterns at four signed amplitudes on one 16-cubed grid; they are not 128 new simulations or evidence of large-grid physical training.

**Reproduction.** Use the original typed train recipe/config/runtime, full source witness, bound input slots, preprocessing and seeded initial inventory. Manufactured feedback is regenerated by the captured analytical.py from the training frame shape/scalars/correction amplitude and its private RNG seed. Historical training ends with CPU deployment/parity forwards; a clean GPU-only reproducer must first be qualified under the current policy. Keep old-Rheon data separate from new-solver captures. No clean retraining, optimizer resume or new-Rheon regeneration is qualified by this review.
