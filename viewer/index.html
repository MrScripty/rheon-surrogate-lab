What was measured

This view replays two independently simulated trajectories from the same initial conditions and forcing. Every AI step uses its own previous velocity and tracer, with a trained pressure prediction replacing the iterative pressure solve. Rheon supplies the other stages. CPU-only mode gives every method eight native CPU threads and the model eight PyTorch CPU threads. GPU mode shows the earlier GPU-model recording, with one native CPU thread. Recorded frame calculation times exclude rendering and capture serialization. The benchmark above includes native startup, all stages, model inference, transfers, acceptance gates and teardown with a resident model. It excludes Python/model initialization and rendering.

This fixed-box experiment has no free liquid surface, variable density, obstacles or viscosity. Accuracy is measured against the numerical reference; visual agreement alone is insufficient.
